Field notes · Editorial philosophy

Buying advice should be specific about uncertainty.

We do not present unverified personal testing as fact. Our pages distinguish verified specifications, editorial assessment and the practical questions a buyer should consider.

That means clear coverage of comfort, sizing, materials, intended use, weather suitability, value and alternatives—without invented star ratings or sales pressure.
