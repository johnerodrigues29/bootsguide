Editorial policy

How BootsGuide earns trust.

Our articles distinguish verified specifications, editorial interpretation and hands-on experience. A reader should always be able to tell which kind of claim they are reading.

PrinciplesResearchReviewsRecommendationsImagesAffiliatesUpdates

Core principles

  • Be precise about fit, materials, weather claims and limitations.
  • Use primary sources for product specifications whenever available.
  • Do not invent ownership, wear tests, measurements or star ratings.
  • Explain who a model is for and who should choose something else.
  • Keep commercial links separate from editorial conclusions.

Source hierarchy

Product pages, official fit guides, manufacturer care instructions and current UK retailer information are the first sources for model-level facts. Secondary sources may add context, but do not override a current primary specification without an explicit reason.

Each detailed guide identifies the most important sources and shows the last fact-check date.

What “review” means on BootsGuide

A BootsGuide review is a structured buying assessment. It evaluates the official construction, fit guidance, use case and alternatives. If an article is based on desk research, it says so. If first-hand testing is added later, the page will explain the pair, test conditions and limitations rather than retroactively implying experience.

How models are recommended

We do not choose a universal winner when needs conflict. A smooth-leather model may be best for smart-casual wear; a terrain-focused sole may be better for mixed paths; an insulated model may be best for cold weather. Tables and verdicts make the deciding trade-off visible.

Price and availability change. We avoid fabricating live prices or discount claims and advise readers to check the current listing.

Product imagery

Where product identification matters, BootsGuide uses official manufacturer or authorised retailer photography. Current Blundstone images are compressed locally to WebP for performance, given descriptive alternative text and credited on the page. Product imagery is used to illustrate editorial coverage; all brand and product rights remain with their respective owners.

Affiliate independence

An affiliate relationship may generate revenue after a qualifying purchase, but it does not decide which specifications are reported or remove a material drawback. Links that earn commission should be labelled and use appropriate sponsored-link attributes.

Updates and corrections

Core guides are reviewed when a product specification, size instruction or care claim changes. Corrections should be made promptly and the fact-check date updated. Grammar or formatting fixes that do not change meaning do not require a new editorial conclusion.
