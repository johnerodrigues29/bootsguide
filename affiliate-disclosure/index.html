Affiliate disclosure

How BootsGuide may earn a commission.

Some product links are affiliate links. A qualifying purchase can support the site without increasing the price you pay.

Affiliate relationships

BootsGuide participates in retailer affiliate programmes, including programmes that may pay commission when a reader follows a tracked link and completes a qualifying purchase. Current affiliate links use Amazon UK; future links may also use specialist boot retailers. Each retailer determines eligibility, attribution and commission.

What this means for readers

Using an affiliate link should not change the price you pay. The site may receive a small share from the retailer. Editorial articles include a disclosure at the end, and outbound commercial links use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" attributes.

Editorial independence

Revenue potential does not justify a false product claim, fictional testing, hidden limitation, fabricated discount or urgency message. We report relevant trade-offs and compare alternatives even when one option may generate commission.

Prices and availability

Retail prices, stock, colours, sizes and offers change quickly. Unless a price is explicitly timestamped and verified, readers should treat the retailer’s current product page and checkout as authoritative. BootsGuide does not sell or fulfil products.
