Commercial transparency

Affiliate disclosure

BootsGuide may earn a commission when you purchase through links on our site. This does not affect the price you pay.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

How affiliate links work

Some BootsGuide pages may contain tracked links to retailers. If you click one and make a qualifying purchase, the retailer may pay BootsGuide a commission. The retailer sets the price and handles the transaction; the commission is not an extra charge added to your order.

Amazon disclosure

BootsGuide may use Amazon UK affiliate links after programme enrolment. When links are activated, the disclosure required by the relevant affiliate programme will appear here and near qualifying links. No Amazon affiliate links are currently active.

Editorial independence

Affiliate eligibility does not make a product suitable for every reader. Guides should explain evidence, buyer fit and trade-offs whether or not a commission is available. Payment must not be used to invent a product test, rating or endorsement.

Prices and availability

Prices and stock can change. BootsGuide does not guarantee that a retailer listing remains available or that a displayed offer is current. Confirm the price, seller, delivery terms and returns policy on the retailer’s own site before ordering.

Brand relationships

BootsGuide is independent and is not an official website of the brands discussed. Trade marks belong to their respective owners.
