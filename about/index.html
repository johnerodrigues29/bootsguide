About BootsGuide

Advice built around the decision, not the click.

BootsGuide is an independent editorial project for UK shoppers comparing boots. We aim to make specifications, fit information and model differences easier to understand.

What we publish

BootsGuide publishes model reviews, side-by-side comparisons and buying guides. The site begins with a focused Blundstone content cluster before expanding to other boot brands.

How we handle product experience

We do not claim first-hand testing unless a product has actually been tested by BootsGuide. Desk research, manufacturer specifications, independent published testing and customer-feedback patterns should be described for what they are.

How affiliate links work

Some links may be affiliate links. If a reader buys through one of those links, BootsGuide may earn a commission without increasing the price paid by the reader.

Editorial goal

A useful page should make a decision easier: what the product is good at, what its limitations are, who it suits and what alternatives are worth considering.
