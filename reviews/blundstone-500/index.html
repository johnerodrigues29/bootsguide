Blundstone 500 Review: Fit, Break-In & Value

The #500 is the foundation of Blundstone’s Original series: an unlined Stout Brown Chelsea boot with a firmer new-boot feel and fewer comfort extras than the Classics. That simplicity is exactly why some buyers prefer it.

Quick verdict

The simpler, firmer Original

The Blundstone 500 is best for buyers who want the iconic round-toe shape in smooth Stout Brown leather and do not need the lining or extra footbed technology of the Classics. It feels firmer at first than the 585, but the unlined leather offers a straightforward boot that can mould with wear.

Best for

Everyday casual wear, buyers who prefer an unlined boot and those wanting the core Original design.

Skip it if

You want a relaxed fit immediately, plush lining, full waterproofing or technical trail support.

Key strengths

  • Classic Stout Brown finish
  • Unlined for simpler feel
  • 2.5 mm water-resistant leather
  • Removable footbed

Trade-offs

  • Firmer initial fit
  • More break-in than Classics
  • Basic EVA footbed
  • Not fully waterproof

Fit and break-in

The Originals fit is firm across the top and toe area when new. Over time, the thicker leather is expected to soften and mould over the flex point. It will not become longer. Start with about 1 cm of toe room, assess the instep while standing and expect only slight heel lift.

If the length is right but the forefoot feels too narrow, compare the corresponding half size because Blundstone half sizes add width. If the boot is short, a half size will not fix it; move to the next full size.

Break-in rule: stiffness that gradually eases is different from toes hitting the front, sharp pressure or numbness. Do not use break-in as a reason to keep the wrong size.

Comfort compared with the Classics

The 500 uses a removable contoured EVA footbed and Blundstone’s shock-protection design, but it does not include the leather-and-textile lining or Poron XRD® footbed specified for the 550 and 585. Buyers who prioritise a softer first wear or a roomier instep should compare the Classics before deciding.

The unlined build may appeal to buyers who prefer less bulk and a more direct leather feel. Comfort remains foot-specific: the simpler model is not automatically less comfortable once properly fitted.

Rain, winter and walking

The 2.5 mm full-grain leather is water-resistant and suitable for ordinary changeable weather with the right care. It is not a waterproof membrane boot, and the elastic panels and opening limit protection in deep water. Let wet boots dry naturally and never speed the process with a radiator.

For everyday walking on streets and easy paths, the light Chelsea format is practical. For slippery mud, long wet routes or uneven ground, compare the All-Terrain series or a dedicated walking boot.

Durability and care

Clean dried dirt with a soft brush or damp cloth, air dry away from heat and use a suitable full-grain leather product after testing it on a hidden section. Blundstone states that boot lifespan varies with frequency, surface, exposure and care; its general two-to-seven-year estimate is not a guarantee.

Is the Blundstone 500 good value?

The 500 offers the central Blundstone design without paying for the lined Classics specification. It is the logical value choice if you prefer its firmer fit and smooth Stout Brown upper. The 585 is worth the step up when the relaxed fit, waxed nubuck and upgraded footbed are features you will appreciate every day.

Frequently asked questions

Do Blundstone 500 boots run true to size?

Blundstone says the Original 500 usually runs true to UK size, with a firm fit over the top and toe when new. Half sizes increase width rather than length.

How long does the Blundstone 500 take to break in?

There is no fixed period. The thicker unlined leather is expected to soften and mould over time, but the boot should be the correct length from day one because it will not stretch longer.

Is the Blundstone 500 waterproof?

Its 2.5 mm leather is water-resistant, not fully waterproof. It can handle ordinary rain with care but is not intended for prolonged saturation.

What is the difference between Blundstone 500 and 585?

The 500 is an unlined Original with a firmer initial fit and contoured EVA footbed. The 585 is a lined Classic with waxed nubuck, a more relaxed fit and an XRD-equipped removable footbed.
