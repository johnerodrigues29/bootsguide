Blundstone 585 Review

A practical assessment of the rustic brown Classic, based on published product information — with the strengths, limits and fit questions worth checking before buying.

Our assessment

Quick verdict

The 585 is the rustic-looking member of Blundstone’s Classics family. On the published specification, it is best suited to buyers who want an easy pull-on Chelsea boot, a relaxed Classic-series fit and waxed nubuck that is intended to develop character rather than stay uniformly polished.

Best for: everyday casual wear and buyers who prefer a textured rustic brown finish with the Classic-series lining and removable footbed.

Strengths

  • Water-resistant waxed nubuck upper
  • Leather and textile lining
  • Removable contoured footbed
  • Pull-on design with familiar Chelsea-boot versatility

Trade-offs

  • Not a fully waterproof boot
  • Rustic leather naturally varies in colour
  • Half sizes affect width rather than length
  • Not designed as a specialist hiking or safety boot

Design & construction

The 585 follows the familiar elastic-sided, pull-on Chelsea-boot format. Its defining visual difference is the Rustic Brown waxed nubuck upper: a textured finish with natural colour variation rather than the more uniform appearance of smooth leather.

Published construction details include double stitching, a leather-and-textile lining and the brand’s Classic sole unit. Those features place the 585 in the Classics range rather than the simpler Originals range. For a buyer, the useful question is not whether “Classic” sounds better, but whether the added lining and footbed specification justify the model choice.

Comfort

On paper, comfort is one of the 585’s clearer priorities. The model uses a contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD technology and a PU midsole, while the footbed is removable. That gives buyers a practical way to adjust volume or use a compatible alternative insole.

What the specification cannot tell you

Shock-absorption components do not guarantee comfort for every foot. Instep height, heel shape, sock thickness and walking pattern still matter. Treat published comfort features as a reason to try the boot, not proof that it will fit you.

Fit & sizing

Blundstone’s UK guidance describes the 585 as true to size with a relatively relaxed feel. The important sizing quirk is that a Blundstone half size adds width and volume rather than length. If your toes need more room front to back, moving up a full size — not merely a half size — is the relevant adjustment according to the brand’s general guidance.

Try the boots standing up, with the socks you plan to wear. The brand advises leaving roughly 1 cm ahead of the toes and notes that slight heel lift can be normal. The upper may ease over the instep with use, but buyers should not expect the boot to stretch in length.

Materials

Waxed nubuck is the central reason to choose the 585 over a smooth-leather alternative. It gives the boot a matte, broken-in appearance, but it also means pairs can vary and marks may become part of the finish. Buyers who want a consistent polished surface may prefer a smooth full-grain model such as the 550.

Durability

The upper, double stitching and TPU outsole are all durability-oriented features. Actual service life, however, depends on frequency of wear, surfaces, moisture exposure, drying and care. BootsGuide does not assign a lifespan based on the specification alone.

Regular cleaning and material-appropriate conditioning are more useful than a blanket durability promise. Nubuck needs care products suitable for its finish; smooth-leather creams can change its appearance.

UK weather performance

The 585 upper is described as water-resistant, not waterproof. That distinction matters in the UK: the boot may cope with ordinary showers and damp pavements when cared for, but it is not a substitute for a sealed waterproof boot during prolonged exposure.

If the upper becomes wet, the manufacturer recommends natural drying at room temperature rather than direct artificial heat. Weatherproofing products should be checked for nubuck compatibility and tested discreetly because treatments can alter colour.

Everyday wear

The pull-on shape and understated brown colour make the 585 easy to pair with casual clothes. The tread and construction appear oriented towards general day-to-day use, not technical hiking, deep mud or certified workplace protection. Match the boot to the job: versatility is useful, but it is not the same as specialist performance.

Value for money

Value depends on whether you specifically want the Classics features. If the lining, removable contoured footbed and rustic nubuck finish matter to you, the 585 presents a coherent package. If you mainly want the basic Blundstone silhouette, comparing the Classics with an Originals model may reveal features you do not need.

Who should buy it?

  • Buyers drawn to a rustic brown, textured leather finish.
  • People seeking a general-purpose pull-on boot for everyday casual wear.
  • Those who value a lined upper and removable contoured footbed.
  • Buyers willing to follow material-specific care guidance.

Who should skip it?

  • Anyone needing guaranteed waterproof protection.
  • Buyers requiring a certified work or safety boot.
  • People who prefer a uniform, polishable smooth-leather finish.
  • Anyone unable to try or return a fit-sensitive pull-on boot.

Alternatives

The closest decision is the Blundstone 550: it shares the Classics platform but uses Walnut Brown full-grain leather rather than Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. The choice is therefore driven less by a “better” model and more by finish, care preference and appearance.

Final verdict

Based on the verified specification, the Blundstone 585 is a sensible candidate for buyers who want the Classics fit and comfort components in a deliberately rustic finish. Its limits are equally clear: water-resistant is not waterproof, fit remains personal and the textured nubuck needs appropriate care. If those trade-offs suit your routine, it belongs on the shortlist.
