Blundstone 585 Review: Fit, Comfort & Value

The #585 is the rustic-brown Classic many people picture when they think of Blundstone. This review explains what the official specifications mean in practice—and where this easy everyday Chelsea boot is not the right tool.

By BootsGuide Editorial 21 October 2026
Quick verdict

A casual Chelsea to shortlist after a careful fit check

Our buying recommendation is to shortlist the 585 if you want a rustic brown boot for everyday outfits and its shape suits your feet. Choose it for the finish and a successful indoor fitting, rather than a promise of instant comfort. We have not personally wear-tested this pair or measured cushioning, grip or lifespan.

Best for

Everyday wear, city walking, travel and buyers who like a rustic brown finish.

Skip it if

You need full waterproofing, aggressive hiking traction, safety certification or firm ankle support.

Key strengths

  • Relaxed Classics fit
  • Water-resistant waxed nubuck
  • Removable comfort footbed
  • Easy pull-on design

Trade-offs

  • Not fully waterproof
  • Rustic leather varies naturally
  • Limited ankle lockdown

The important distinction is the upper: “rustic leather” here means waxed nubuck, not smooth full-grain leather. Natural shade and grain variation are part of the finish, so a delivered pair may not look identical to a studio photograph.

Leather, design and everyday versatility

Our styling preference is to wear the 585 with straight jeans, olive trousers and casual layers. Decide whether you like a varied rustic appearance before ordering; a uniform polished finish is a different buying goal.

The official page notes that natural colour differences can make a delivered pair look different from studio images. Compare the selected model description as well as the photograph. Do not assume that every brown Blundstone listing is the same boot.

Comfort, fit and sizing

The official size guide recommends about 1 cm of toe room and explains that a little heel lift can be normal. Half sizes add width and space over the foot, not length. The leather may ease over the instep but will not lengthen.

An indoor fitting checklist

  1. Try both boots with the socks you intend to use, on a clean surface that preserves return eligibility.
  2. Stand and walk before judging the fit. Check for toes contacting the front and painful pressure over the instep.
  3. Turn and stop. If you grip with your toes to hold the boot on, reassess the size or shape.
  4. Recheck the fit if you change footbeds or sock thickness; adding padding can also reduce useful space.

Our advice is to compare sizes rather than automatically sizing up or down. A roomier boot is not always more comfortable, and a tight pair is not automatically correct because leather can soften. See our Blundstone sizing guide for more detail.

Break-in expectations

Introduce a correctly fitting pair gradually before a long commute or holiday walking day. Persistent rubbing, numbness or toe pressure is a reason to reassess fit. We cannot promise a fixed break-in period or an all-day comfort result for your feet.

Are Blundstone 585 boots waterproof?

The UK specification describes water-resistant waxed nubuck, not a fully waterproof boot. Our recommendation is to assess the 585 as everyday footwear, rather than choosing it for prolonged wet exposure, standing water or a guaranteed dry-foot result.

For a cold-season alternative, read the 566 vs 585 comparison. It explains why insulation, water protection and grip are separate decisions. Neither an insulated label nor a substantial-looking sole establishes performance on ice.

See our Blundstone waterproofing guide before buying primarily for wet conditions.

Everyday walking: assess your own route

We would consider the 585 for commuting, errands and sightseeing if it passes an indoor fit check. That is a buying suggestion, not a measured walking test. Do not infer long-distance comfort from the footbed name alone.

For technical paths, steep ground or a specific traction requirement, start with those demands. A pull-on Chelsea cannot be adjusted with laces. Our 2056 vs 585 comparison is a useful starting point for exploring another outsole design, without assuming that either boot is suitable for every route.

Care for the waxed nubuck finish

Blundstone’s care guide treats waxed nubuck separately from plain nubuck. It recommends removing dirt with a damp cloth, allowing the boots to dry, and using compatible Rustic Renovating Cream and waterproof spray. Test a new treatment discreetly for changes in colour or appearance; keep drying boots away from direct heat.

Our practical advice is to plan for cleaning and drying between wears. A care product does not turn this model into a different waterproof construction. Read our cleaning guide for the broader routine.

Is the Blundstone 585 worth it?

Price reference checked 21 October 2026: the official UK product page lists £185. This is a manufacturer reference, not an Amazon price or a stock guarantee. We could not confirm live Amazon availability during this update.

The 585 earns its premium when you value the Classics fit, lining and rustic finish enough to wear it frequently. It is harder to justify if you need only an occasional rain boot or if your main use is serious hiking. In those cases, a specialised waterproof or walking boot is a better allocation of the budget.

Before buying, compare the 585 with the same-colour Blundstone 1306 Dress if a longer chisel toe and smarter sole appeal, the smooth black Classic in our Blundstone 558 vs 585 comparison, the Rustic Black 587, the smooth-leather Walnut Brown 550, the more basic unlined 500 and the Vibram-equipped 2056 All-Terrain. The right alternative depends on whether shape, leather finish, fit family or outsole capability matters most.

Frequently asked questions

Do Blundstone 585 boots run true to size?

Use the UK size guide as a starting point and try the actual pair. Half sizes add width rather than length; check toe room, instep pressure and heel hold.

Are Blundstone 585 boots waterproof?

The official UK page describes water-resistant uppers. Do not treat this as a guarantee of waterproof protection during prolonged wet exposure.

Do Blundstone 585 boots need breaking in?

Allow a gradual introduction, but do not expect break-in to fix insufficient length or painful pressure. No fixed comfort timeline is promised.

Is the Blundstone 585 good for walking?

It is an option to try for everyday routes if the fit works. BootsGuide has not performed a walking test or measured grip or fatigue.

Should I choose the 566 instead for winter?
