Blundstone 585 Review: Fit, Comfort & Value

The #585 is the rustic-brown Classic many people picture when they think of Blundstone. This review explains what the official specifications mean in practice—and where this easy everyday Chelsea boot is not the right tool.

By BootsGuide EditorialFact-checked and updated 20 August 2026

Quick verdict

A comfortable all-rounder with a relaxed fit

The Blundstone 585 is a strong choice for buyers who want one pull-on boot for commuting, travel, weekends and everyday errands. Its waxed nubuck upper gives it a softer, more casual appearance than the smooth-leather 550, while the leather-and-textile lining, removable XRD® footbed and Classics fit make it the more comfort-focused alternative to the unlined 500.

Best for

Everyday wear, city walking, travel and buyers who like a rustic brown finish.

Skip it if

You need full waterproofing, aggressive hiking traction, safety certification or firm ankle support.

Key strengths

  • Relaxed Classics fit
  • Water-resistant waxed nubuck
  • Removable comfort footbed
  • Easy pull-on design

Trade-offs

  • Not fully waterproof
  • Rustic leather varies naturally
  • Limited ankle lockdown
  • Premium price versus Originals

The important distinction is the upper: “rustic leather” here means waxed nubuck, not smooth full-grain leather. Natural shade and grain variation are part of the finish, so a delivered pair may not look identical to a studio photograph.

Leather, design and everyday versatility

The #585 keeps the familiar round-toe Chelsea silhouette, elastic side panels and two pull tabs. The muted Rustic Brown colour works particularly well with denim, chinos, workwear-inspired layers and casual winter clothing. Scuffs tend to blend into the mottled finish more naturally than on polished smooth leather, although the surface still needs material-appropriate care.

The boot is built around a durable TPU outsole and shock-absorbing midsole rather than a welted, easily resoleable dress-boot construction. That makes it light and practical for daily use, but buyers specifically seeking traditional stitched construction should compare other boot categories.

Comfort, fit and sizing

Blundstone describes the Classics as relaxed across the top and toe and says the 585 runs true to size. That does not mean every foot should order its usual trainer size: use the UK size stamped on the outsole, try the socks you intend to wear and assess length before width.

A correctly sized pair should leave roughly 1 cm in front of the toes. Slight heel lift is normal in a pull-on Chelsea boot; toes pressing the front is not. Crucially, Blundstone half sizes increase width, not length. If a UK 8 is long enough but tight across the forefoot or instep, an 8.5 is the relevant comparison. If it is too short, move to the next full size.

Fit check: the leather may ease over the instep, but it will not stretch longer. Do not keep a pair that relies on the toe box lengthening with wear.

Break-in expectations

The waxed nubuck and leather lining are intended to feel more forgiving than the thicker, unlined Original #500. A short period of indoor wear can still reveal pressure at the instep or heel, but severe rubbing or numbness is a sizing warning, not a normal break-in target.

Are the Blundstone 585 waterproof?

No—not in the strict sense. The official UK listing describes the upper as water-resistant, and Blundstone says leather footwear should not be exposed to moisture for prolonged periods. The 585 is suitable for ordinary British drizzle, damp pavements and brief showers when maintained, but elastic panels, the opening and sustained saturation remain limitations.

For wet use, remove surface dirt, let the boots dry naturally at room temperature and use a product suitable for waxed nubuck. Avoid radiators and direct heat, which can dry and damage leather. If your routine involves standing water or long wet walks, choose a genuinely waterproof boot or a weatherproof Thermal model instead.

Read our full Blundstone waterproofing guide for the difference between water-resistant and waterproof models.

Are Blundstone 585 boots good for walking?

For commuting, sightseeing, dog walks on firm paths and general day-to-day mileage, the 585’s cushioning, low weight and simple pull-on shape make sense. The removable footbed also lets some buyers fine-tune volume or use a compatible orthotic.

For technical hiking, the limitations are clearer: there are no laces to lock the heel, the ankle is not braced like a hiking boot, and the Classic outsole is less terrain-focused than Blundstone’s Vibram-equipped All-Terrain range. Choose the task first. A comfortable lifestyle boot can be excellent for ordinary walking without being the best boot for a mountain route.

Durability and care

How long a 585 lasts depends on rotation, surfaces, moisture, storage and care. Blundstone gives a broad two-to-seven-year expectation for average wear across its boots, explicitly not as a service-life guarantee. Keeping the upper clean and allowing wet boots to dry naturally are more useful than chasing a fixed lifespan.

For Rustic Brown waxed nubuck, Blundstone recommends its non-pigmented Rustic Renovating Cream and waterproof spray after first testing on an inconspicuous area. Do not automatically apply smooth-leather polish; the wrong product can change colour and texture.

Is the Blundstone 585 worth it?

The 585 earns its premium when you value the Classics fit, lining and rustic finish enough to wear it frequently. It is harder to justify if you need only an occasional rain boot or if your main use is serious hiking. In those cases, a specialised waterproof or walking boot is a better allocation of the budget.

Before buying, compare the 585 with the smooth-leather 550 and the more basic unlined 500. Those three models share a recognisable silhouette but feel and age differently.

Frequently asked questions

Do Blundstone 585 boots run true to size?

Blundstone says the 585 generally runs true to UK size. A half size adds width rather than extra length, and the Classics fit is more relaxed across the top and toe than the Originals.

Are Blundstone 585 boots waterproof?

The 585 uses water-resistant waxed nubuck, but Blundstone does not recommend prolonged exposure to moisture. It is better described as weather-ready for normal everyday rain than fully waterproof.

Do Blundstone 585 boots need breaking in?

The manufacturer describes the 585 as having a relaxed, worn-in feel from the first wear. The waxed nubuck can still soften over time, but the model should not need the same firm break-in often associated with the unlined Originals.

Is the Blundstone 585 good for walking?

It can work well for everyday urban walking and easy paths when the fit is right. For long hikes, steep or muddy terrain, or heavy ankle support, a dedicated walking boot is the safer choice.
