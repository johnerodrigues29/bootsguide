Blundstone 1609 Review: Fit, Comfort & Value

The #1609 is a premium Classics Chelsea boot in Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather. It keeps the relaxed fit, leather lining and XRD-equipped footbed that make the Classics popular, while giving buyers a smoother and more refined brown finish than the 585.

By BootsGuide EditorialPublished 6 October 2026 · Updated 14 October 2026
Quick verdict

A more polished Classic, not a different kind of boot

The Blundstone 1609 makes the most sense for buyers who already like the Classics formula but want a brown leather finish that looks cleaner and more deliberate than the mottled character of the 585. Its Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather, leather-and-textile lining and cushioned footbed make it a strong everyday option for commuting, travel and smart-casual wardrobes.

The important point is that the 1609 is still fundamentally a Classic. It is not a technical rain boot, a hiking boot or a radically different fit. Blundstone lists it as true to size, with half sizes providing extra width, and the relaxed lined construction is designed to feel comfortable quickly.

Best for

Smart-casual everyday wear, commuting, travel and buyers who prefer smooth brown leather.

Consider first

The £195 price is easier to justify if you specifically prefer the Antique Brown leather over the £185 585.

Key strengths

  • Premium waxed full-grain leather
  • Relaxed Classics fit
  • Leather-and-textile lining
  • Contoured XRD® comfort footbed

Trade-offs

  • Not fully waterproof
  • No lace-based heel adjustment
  • Premium price within the Classics range

Fit, sizing and comfort

The 1609 follows the relaxed Classics fit rather than the firmer Originals shape. Blundstone says it runs true to size and recommends a half size when extra width is needed. That distinction matters: the half size is not intended to make the boot substantially longer.

The leather lining gives the boot a softer first-wear feel than an unlined Original. The contoured EVA footbed also incorporates Poron XRD® technology for impact absorption. For a pull-on Chelsea, a small amount of heel movement can be normal; what matters is that the heel settles without excessive slipping and that your longest toe has usable space without pressing the front.

If you already own a 585, the 1609 should feel familiar because both sit in the Classics family. If your current pair is an Original such as the 500 or 510, expect a more relaxed, cushioned starting point rather than simply the same fit in a different colour.

Best sizing approach: start with your usual UK Blundstone size, then use the half-size option if you need more width. Try the boots indoors with the socks you expect to wear most often and pay attention to heel security as well as toe room.

Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather

The 1609's biggest visual selling point is its Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather. Compared with the Rustic Brown waxed nubuck used on the 585, the surface looks smoother and generally reads as more refined. That makes the 1609 easier to place with dark denim, chinos, smart-casual trousers and other outfits where a very rugged finish might feel too casual.

The finish should still be treated as leather rather than as a dress shoe. Natural variation and wear will change its appearance over time, and small marks are part of the ownership story. The advantage is that the 1609 starts with a relatively clean surface while retaining the casual practicality of a Chelsea boot.

Comfort for everyday wear

Blundstone's Classic platform is built around cushioning rather than a lightweight minimalist feel. The removable contoured EVA footbed uses Poron XRD® technology, while the TPU outsole and PU midsole provide the underlying structure. The leather-and-textile lining also helps explain why the 1609 is positioned as a more comfortable, premium-feeling option within the range.

For office commutes, shopping, travel and long days moving between pavement and indoor spaces, those features make sense. Comfort is still highly individual, though: a well-cushioned boot that is too loose or too narrow will not become comfortable simply because the materials are premium.

Is the Blundstone 1609 good in rain?

The short answer is: good for ordinary wet weather, not waterproof. Blundstone describes the upper as water-resistant and recommends its broader care approach for maintaining leather. That makes the 1609 a practical choice for UK rain, damp pavements and normal outdoor use, but it should not be treated as a sealed waterproof boot.

The elastic sides are also part of the equation. Avoid standing water for extended periods, dry wet boots naturally at room temperature and use products appropriate for the leather. If your main requirement is maximum winter weather protection, a Thermal model such as the Blundstone 566 or a weather-focused model may be a better fit.

Walking, commuting and travel

The 1609 is a good everyday walking boot rather than a hiking boot. Its pull-on construction is convenient at airports and during travel, while the cushioning platform makes ordinary city mileage and standing practical. The Classic outsole provides useful everyday traction, but the boot is not designed around technical trail grip.

For rougher mixed surfaces, the Blundstone 2056 adds an All-Terrain Vibram outsole and is the more purpose-built option. For steep or technical hikes, a laced hiking boot still provides better lockdown and terrain-specific support.

How the 1609 compares with the 585 and 550

The closest question is usually not whether the 1609 is a better boot, but which Classic finish fits your wardrobe.

1609 vs 585

The 585 uses Rustic Brown waxed nubuck and has a more visibly rugged, naturally varied appearance. The 1609 uses Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather and looks smoother. Both are lined Classics with a similar comfort platform. Choose by leather character and styling first.

1609 vs 550

The 550 is the Walnut Brown member of the same Classic family and is another strong smart-casual choice. The decision is primarily about colour and leather finish rather than a major difference in fit or comfort. The 1609 costs £10 more at the official UK prices checked on 14 October 2026. Read the full 1609 vs 550 comparison to decide whether the finish justifies the difference.

1609 vs 1306

The 1306 is the more distinctive choice if you want a longer chisel-toe Dress Series silhouette. The 1609 is rounder, more traditionally casual and easier to treat as an all-purpose Classic.

Is the Blundstone 1609 worth £195?

At the official UK price checked on 14 October 2026, the 1609 sits £10 above the 585 and 550 and £20 above the Originals 500 and 510. The premium is not buying a completely different comfort system. It is mainly buying the particular Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather and the way that finish positions the Classic boot in a smarter wardrobe.

That makes the 1609 a good value proposition for someone who expects to wear one pair across commuting, weekends, travel and casual social settings. It is less compelling if your priority is simply getting the Classic construction for the lowest price, because the 585 and 550 cover much of the same functional ground.

Care and maintenance

Because the 1609 uses waxed full-grain leather, care should preserve the leather rather than trying to make it look artificially glossy. Remove surface dirt gently, allow damp boots to dry naturally and use a suitable leather-care product in moderation. Always test a new product on a discreet area first because conditioners and polishes can change the colour or finish.

Keep the removable footbeds dry when possible and avoid direct heat such as radiators or hairdryers. Good care will not turn the 1609 into a waterproof boot, but it can help maintain the leather's water resistance and appearance over time.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Blundstone 1609 worth the extra cost over the 585?

The 1609 is £10 more at the official UK price checked on 14 October 2026. Both are Classics with a leather-and-textile lining, XRD-equipped footbed and water-resistant upper. The 1609 is worth the premium mainly if you prefer its Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather and smoother, more refined appearance.

Does the Blundstone 1609 run true to size?

Blundstone UK says the 1609 runs true to size and recommends ordering a half size for a wider fit. As with other Classics, half sizes are about width rather than extra length. Check heel security and toe room rather than choosing solely by the number on your current boots.

Is the Blundstone 1609 waterproof?

No. Blundstone describes the leather as water-resistant, not waterproof. It is appropriate for normal rain and damp urban use when cared for, but prolonged saturation can still penetrate the upper, elastic and seams.

What is the difference between the Blundstone 1609 and 585?

Both are lined Classics, but the 1609 uses Antique Brown waxed full-grain leather while the 585 uses Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. The 1609 looks smoother and more polished; the 585 has a more visibly rustic, broken-in character.

Is the Blundstone 1609 good for walking?

Yes for commuting, travel, city walking and ordinary everyday mileage when the fit is secure. It is not a substitute for a laced hiking boot or an All-Terrain model on steep, technical or persistently muddy terrain.

Before you order: fit, listing and returns

Use the model code #1609 Antique Brown when checking a listing. A page may offer several sizes or colours: confirm the selected option, seller, total delivery cost and return terms rather than relying on its first photograph.

Blundstone’s UK size guide advises approximately 1cm ahead of the toes and notes that leather can ease across the instep but will not become longer. Try both boots indoors with your normal socks. Slight heel lift is different from a foot that slides around; do not keep a short pair in the hope that break-in will fix its length.

If prolonged wet exposure, ice grip or adjustable ankle hold is essential, broaden the shortlist. The 1609’s everyday water resistance and pull-on construction do not establish those capabilities.
