Blundstone 550 Review: Fit, Leather & Comfort

The #550 combines the roomier, lined Classics construction with smooth Walnut Brown full-grain leather. It is the polished alternative to the rustic 585—not a completely different boot underneath.

By BootsGuide Editorial 29 September 2026
Quick verdict

The smart-casual Classic

Choose the Blundstone 550 if you like the comfort and relaxed fit of the Classics series but want smooth full-grain leather that can be dressed more neatly than the 585’s waxed nubuck. The two models share their lining, footbed and sole specification; appearance and care routine should drive the decision.

Best for

Smart-casual everyday wear, commuting, travel and buyers who prefer smooth brown leather.

Consider first

It still is not fully waterproof, and smooth leather shows scratches differently from rustic nubuck.

Key strengths

  • Relaxed Classics fit
  • Leather-and-textile lining
  • Removable XRD® footbed
  • Versatile Walnut Brown

Trade-offs

  • No lace adjustment
  • Not a technical hiking boot
  • Needs smooth-leather care
  • Premium over the 500

Fit, comfort and break-in

The #550 uses the relaxed Classics last: more room over the top of the foot and toe area than the Original #500, plus a leather-and-textile lining. Blundstone describes it as true to size and comfortable from the outset. The extra supplied footbeds can help reduce volume if the fit feels generous.

Allow about 1 cm between your longest toe and the front of the boot. A small amount of heel movement is expected because Chelsea boots lack laces; excessive lift or toes contacting the front indicates the wrong fit. A half size is for width, not length.

Who may prefer the 550 fit? Buyers with a higher instep or who find the Original series firm over the forefoot often have a better starting point in the Classics. That is a useful tendency, not a guarantee for every foot.

Full-grain leather versus the 585’s waxed nubuck

Walnut Brown full-grain leather gives the 550 a smoother, more uniform appearance. It suits a slightly smarter wardrobe and responds to appropriate leather cream or polish. The 585’s Rustic Brown waxed nubuck looks more mottled and hides casual scuffs differently.

Neither finish is universally better. Pick the 550 if you want a cleaner brown surface and are happy to maintain smooth leather. Pick the 585 if you prefer patina, colour variation and a deliberately rugged look. The Blundstone 558 is the closest alternative when you want the 550’s smooth-leather Classics formula in black.

Wet-weather performance

The 550’s leather is described as water-resistant, not fully waterproof. It should handle normal light rain and damp urban use when maintained, but prolonged exposure can overwhelm leather and the elastic-sided construction. Dry wet boots at room temperature, away from heaters, and apply a suitable protective product after testing it on a hidden area.

Walking and all-day wear

The cushioned footbed, shock-protection system and light pull-on design make the 550 well suited to everyday mileage, standing and travel if its shape matches your foot. For muddy trails or steep ground, the All-Terrain #2056 adds a more aggressive Vibram outsole; for long hikes, a laced hiking boot provides better lockdown.

Is the Blundstone 550 worth the premium?

Compared with the 500, the 550 adds a more relaxed fit, lining and the XRD-equipped Classics footbed. The premium makes sense if those details increase how often you will wear it. If you want a firmer, unlined and more traditional Original, the 500 is the more direct buy.

Compared with the 585, value is essentially equal: choose the leather finish you prefer, not a supposed performance winner. The same applies to the black 558, which shares the key Classics comfort and sole specification. If the priority is a sharper brown boot rather than the round-toe Classics shape, compare the Rustic Brown Blundstone 1306 Dress.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Blundstone 550 and 585?

Both are lined Classics with the same broad comfort platform. The main difference is the upper and colour: the 550 uses smooth full-grain leather in Walnut Brown, while the 585 uses waxed nubuck in Rustic Brown.

Does the Blundstone 550 run true to size?

Blundstone says the 550 runs true to UK size and recommends a half size when extra width is needed. Half sizes do not add length.

Is the Blundstone 550 waterproof?

The 550 has a water-resistant leather upper, but it is not designed for prolonged exposure to moisture. Regular suitable care improves resistance.

Is the Blundstone 550 good for wide feet?

The Classics fit is more relaxed than the Originals, and a half size adds width. Fit still depends on your instep and foot shape, so an indoor trial and return-friendly retailer matter.
