Best Blundstone Boots UK: 6 Models Compared

There is no single best Blundstone for every buyer. The useful answer changes with fit, leather, terrain and weather, so we matched six current UK models to the job each is best equipped to do.

1. Blundstone 585 — best overall

The #585 is the most balanced option for a first pair. It combines a relaxed Classics fit, leather-and-textile lining, removable contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD® and water-resistant Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. The mottled finish works with denim and casual layers and does not demand a polished appearance.

Buy it for: commuting, travel, city walking and everyday casual wear. Avoid it for: standing water, technical hiking or jobs that require safety certification.

Read our complete Blundstone 585 review.

2. Blundstone 550 — best smooth-leather Classic

The #550 shares the 585’s lining, relaxed fit, XRD® footbed and sole construction. Its Walnut Brown full-grain leather is the reason to choose it. The surface looks cleaner and slightly smarter, making it easier to pair with chinos or a relaxed workplace wardrobe.

Buy it for: Classic-series comfort with a more polished brown finish. Choose the 585 instead: if you prefer a rustic surface and visible patina.

Compare the 550 and 585 side by side.

3. Blundstone 500 — best Original

The #500 is unlined, uses 2.5 mm full-grain leather and starts firmer across the instep and toe than the Classics. Its contoured EVA footbed is simpler than the 585’s. Buyers who like a boot to soften and mould over time may prefer that direct Original construction.

Buy it for: the iconic core design, smooth Stout Brown and a lower price than the Classics. Choose the 585 instead: if you want more room and less break-in.

Read our Blundstone 500 review.

4. Blundstone 587 — best rustic black option

The #587 is essentially the black counterpart to the 585: Rustic Black waxed nubuck, relaxed Classics fit, leather-and-textile lining and the removable XRD® footbed. It is useful when black works better with your wardrobe but you do not want the uniform surface of smooth leather.

For a smooth black, firmer Original, compare the #510. Do not choose between them by colour alone—the 587 is lined and relaxed, while the 510 is unlined and firm when new.

5. Blundstone 2056 — best for rougher paths

The #2056 All-Terrain keeps Rustic Brown waxed nubuck but swaps in a Vibram rubber outsole designed for cushioning, traction and self-cleaning tread. Blundstone also specifies a leather-and-synthetic lining and water-resistant treated upper.

Buy it for: mixed surfaces, dog walks and buyers who want more grip than the Classic sole. Remember: it is still a pull-on Chelsea boot, not a high-support, fully waterproof hiking boot.

6. Blundstone 566 — best for cold, wet winter use

The #566 Thermal has the most weather-focused specification in this shortlist: water-resistant treated full-grain leather, tight-knit coated elastic, sealed elastic seams, Thinsulate® insulation and a removable sheepswool-lined footbed.

It will feel snugger than a standard Classic because of the insulated footbed. That warmth can be a disadvantage indoors or in milder months, so choose it when winter use is the priority rather than as the default year-round model.

How to choose the right Blundstone

  1. Choose the job: everyday streets, smart-casual wear, rougher paths or winter cold.
  2. Choose the fit family: firm Original or relaxed Classic/All-Terrain.
  3. Choose the upper: smooth full-grain leather or mottled waxed nubuck.
  4. Check length first: half sizes add width, not length.
  5. Match weather claims to reality: water-resistant is not the same as fully waterproof.

The model you will wear twice a week is better value than a theoretically “better” model that does not match your feet or wardrobe.

Frequently asked questions

Which Blundstone is best for everyday wear?

For most everyday buyers, the 585 is the strongest all-round specification because it combines the relaxed Classics fit, lining, removable XRD footbed and versatile Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. The 550 offers the same platform in smoother Walnut Brown leather.

Which Blundstone is best for wide feet?

Blundstone half sizes add width, and the Classics and All-Terrain series have a more relaxed fit than the Originals. Foot shape still matters, so use the official fit guidance and a return-friendly retailer.

Which Blundstone is best for walking?

For ordinary urban walking, the 585 or 550 are comfortable all-round choices. For rougher paths, the 2056 All-Terrain adds a Vibram rubber outsole with more terrain-focused traction.

Which Blundstone is best for winter?

The 566 Thermal has the most winter-focused specification here: water-resistant treated leather, coated elastic with sealed seams, Thinsulate insulation and a sheepswool footbed.

What is the most classic Blundstone model?

The #500 is the foundational Original: unlined Stout Brown full-grain leather, round toe, elastic sides and a durable TPU sole.
