Short answer

Start with finish and series

The 585 is our everyday starting point for buyers drawn to rustic brown nubuck and the lined Classics specification. Choose the 550 for a smoother Walnut Brown Classic, the 500 for the simpler Stout Brown Originals route, or the 510 if that Original specification in black better suits your wardrobe.

What about wide feet?

Do not assume that a particular model number solves width. Blundstone’s UK guidance says its half sizes add width rather than length, while the Classics are described as having a more relaxed fit across the top and toe. That makes fit strategy at least as important as model choice.

A useful sizing rule

If you need more width or instep volume, consider a half size; if you need more length, a full size is the relevant change. Always confirm with an indoor try-on and the retailer’s current returns policy.

Sizing advice

Start with your usual UK size, but do not stop there. Check space at the toes, heel movement, instep pressure and the socks you will use. A pull-on boot needs enough room to flex without feeling uncontrolled. Read the full fit guide for a step-by-step check.

Common questions

FAQ

Which Blundstone is best for everyday wear?

The 585 is a useful starting point if you like rustic brown nubuck and want the Classics specification. The 550 is the close alternative for a smoother finish.

Which is better, the 550 or 585?

Neither is universally better. Their published lining, footbed and sole details closely match; the key difference is full-grain Walnut Brown leather on the 550 versus Rustic Brown waxed nubuck on the 585.

Are Blundstone half sizes longer?

No. The official UK size guide says half sizes add width and volume, not length.

Are standard Blundstones waterproof?

Leather models may be water-resistant, but the brand does not describe natural leather as providing full waterproof protection. Avoid prolonged moisture exposure.

Final recommendation

Begin with the 585 if its rustic finish appeals, then compare it directly with the smoother 550. Move to the 500 or 510 when you specifically prefer the Originals proposition. The right decision is the model whose fit, finish and care needs align with your routine — not the one with the loudest label.
