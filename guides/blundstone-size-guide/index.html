Blundstone Size Guide UK: Fit & Half Sizes Explained

Blundstone uses UK sizing, but its half sizes work differently from many shoe brands: they add width, not length. Use this step-by-step fit check before you decide what to keep.

By BootsGuide Editorial 29 September 2026
The short answer

Start with your normal UK boot size—then fit length before width

Blundstone states that most core models run true to UK size. Aim for roughly 1 cm in front of the toes, expect slight heel lift and use a half size only when you need more width. Leather may ease across the instep, but it will never stretch longer.

How Blundstone half sizes work

This is the most important sizing detail: Blundstone half sizes increase width, not length. A UK 7.5 has the same length as a UK 7 but provides more room across the foot. A UK 8 is the next length up.

Five-step Blundstone fit check

  1. Use the correct reference: Blundstone prints the UK size on the bottom of the boot. Do not compare a US-labelled trainer directly without converting.
  2. Wear your real socks: test with the thickness you expect to wear most often.
  3. Stand up: feet spread under load, so a seated try-on is not enough.
  4. Check toe length: leave approximately 1 cm; toes must not touch the front while walking.
  5. Walk on carpet: assess instep pressure, forefoot width and heel friction while protecting the outsole for returns.
Keep the size if: length is correct, the instep is secure without pain and heel lift is small. Change it if: toes touch, the foot goes numb, pressure is sharp or the heel repeatedly rubs.

These are tendencies, not promises. Material thickness, footbed and your instep shape all influence the final feel. The 500 and 510 receive matching Originals guidance, so their direct comparison is primarily a colour decision. The 558, 550, 585 and 587 share the relaxed Classics guidance, with upper material and finish creating the main differences; our Blundstone 558 vs 585 comparison explains why the two should be sized alike. The #2056 and #585 receive very similar official fit guidance; compare their different sole units in our Blundstone 2056 vs 585 guide.

The Dress Series needs separate treatment. Blundstone describes the #1306 as longer and wider and advises some buyers—particularly those with narrow feet—to consider half or one full size down. Because a generic half-size rule and model-specific advice can point in different directions, try the 1306 indoors with your usual socks and assess heel hold, flex point and instep rather than relying on the number alone.

Common fit problems

“The boot slips at the heel”

Slight lift is normal because there are no laces. If it causes rubbing or feels unstable, try the extra footbed supplied with many Classics, a more appropriate sock or a lower-volume size—without sacrificing toe length.

“The instep is tight”

Leather can ease slightly at its flex point. A firm but comfortable hold may improve; pain, numbness or difficulty inserting the foot suggests the model or width is wrong. Compare a half size or the more relaxed Classics last.

“One foot is larger”

Fit the larger foot for length. An extra footbed, thin volume reducer or sock adjustment can help the smaller foot, provided the retailer and any orthotic professional agree.

“I want to wear thick winter socks”

Try the exact sock before deciding. Do not automatically size up: excess length can create movement even when the sock fills width. Thermal models also have a thicker footbed that changes volume.

Buying online without guessing

  • Use the official UK-size reference, not only an automated conversion widget.
  • Check that the retailer allows indoor try-ons and note the return deadline.
  • Order the two most plausible widths only if the retailer’s policy permits it.
  • Keep packaging and test on clean carpet.
  • Recheck the product’s own “Fit & Size” tab; series guidance can change.

Frequently asked questions

Do Blundstone boots run big or small?

Most Originals and Classics are described as true to UK size, but their volume differs: Originals start firm over the top and toe, while Classics are more relaxed. Foot shape and socks still affect the result.

What do Blundstone half sizes mean?

A Blundstone half size adds width, not length. For example, a UK 8.5 is wider than an 8 but not longer.

How much toe room should Blundstones have?

Blundstone recommends roughly 1 cm from the end of the longest toe to the front of the boot. If your toes touch the front, the boot is too short.

Is heel lift normal in Blundstones?

A slight amount of heel lift is normal in a pull-on Chelsea boot. Large movement, friction or instability may mean the volume or size is wrong.

Will Blundstones stretch?

Leather can stretch slightly over the instep and flex point with use, but the boots will not stretch in length.
