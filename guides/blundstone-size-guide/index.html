Blundstone Size Guide

A practical checklist for using retailer sizing information, thinking through sock thickness and reducing avoidable fit uncertainty before purchase.

By BootsGuide EditorialLast updated 15 August 2026
Start here

Use the retailer’s current size information.

Model availability and size information can change. This guide explains the questions to ask rather than replacing current manufacturer or retailer guidance.

A sensible fitting process

  1. Measure or confirm the size that works in comparable boots.
  2. Choose the sock thickness you expect to wear most often.
  3. Check current UK sizing guidance and the retailer’s returns policy.
  4. Try the boot indoors on a clean surface, following the retailer’s conditions.

What to assess

Fit considerations to assess during a trial fitting
AreaQuestion
Toe boxDo your toes have comfortable space when standing and walking?
HeelIs the heel secure without uncomfortable pressure or excessive movement?
InstepDoes the elastic-sided construction feel secure without creating pressure?
SocksDoes the intended sock thickness change the fit meaningfully?

Half sizes and width

Do not assume that a half-size label represents a predictable length or width change across all models and ranges. Check the retailer’s current explanation and use trial fitting where possible.

Fit note: If wide feet or a high instep are part of the decision, prioritise space and comfort over attempting to force a model into a size that only appears right on paper.

Frequently asked questions

Do Blundstones run big or small?

Fit can vary by model, foot shape, socks and personal preference. Current retailer guidance and a return option are more reliable than a universal rule.

Should I size up for thick socks?

Check fit with the socks you expect to use. Whether a different size is appropriate depends on the boot and your foot shape.
