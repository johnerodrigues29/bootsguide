Are Blundstones Waterproof? Rain Protection by Model

Most leather Blundstones resist everyday showers rather than prolonged soaking. Compare the exact model and the weather you expect before relying on a waterproof claim.

By BootsGuide Editorial 30 October 2026
Quick answer

Weather-ready, yes. Fully waterproof, usually no.

Blundstone says natural leather does not provide complete waterproof protection. Many models use water-resistant leather that can repel ordinary rain, but the brand advises against prolonged moisture exposure. The elastic panels and open ankle are also natural entry points. Treat most Blundstones as everyday weather-resistant boots, not wellingtons.

Water-resistant, weatherproof and waterproof are not interchangeable

How to interpret common boot claims
Claim Practical meaning Suitable use
Water-resistant leather Repels some surface moisture when maintained; not sealed against prolonged wetting Showers, damp pavements, short wet journeys
Weatherproof construction Additional treated materials, coated elastic or sealed details improve protection Colder, wetter everyday conditions
Fully waterproof boot A complete system is designed to stop water under its stated conditions Sustained rain, deep mud or standing water—subject to boot height

A product page should make the claim, not the boot’s appearance. Brown leather and a chunky sole do not prove waterproofing.

How popular Blundstone models compare in rain

#500, #510, #550, #558, #585, #587 and #1306

These popular Original, Classic and Dress models use water-resistant leather. The #500 and smooth black #510 Original share the same published weather claim; our 500 vs 510 comparison confirms that colour does not change it. The smooth black #558 and Rustic Brown #585 also share the same weather limitation despite using different leather finishes; see our 558 vs 585 comparison for the care differences. The #587 Rustic Black and Dress Series #1306 Rustic Brown likewise use water-resistant waxed nubuck rather than a fully waterproof construction. They make sense for everyday British weather with routine care, but none should be bought as a substitute for a membrane hiking boot or wellington.

#2056 All-Terrain

The #2056 adds water-resistant treated waxed nubuck and a Vibram rubber outsole with terrain-focused grip. The sole helps on mixed surfaces, but “All-Terrain” does not eliminate the opening and elastic-panel limitations of a Chelsea boot. #566 Thermal

The #566 has a stronger winter specification: treated full-grain leather, tight-knit coated elastic, sealed elastic seams, Thinsulate® and a sheepswool footbed. It is the logical choice here when warmth and improved wet-weather protection matter most. Read the full Blundstone 566 review for its winter value and limits.

Can you wear Blundstones in UK rain?

Yes, if the rain is part of normal everyday use and the boots are maintained. Short commutes, damp high streets and a quick walk in a shower fit the water-resistant claim. Long wet grass, deep puddles, stream crossings and hours of rain do not.

  • Keep water below the elastic and ankle opening.
  • Re-treat the correct leather before protection visibly declines.
  • Choose tread for the surface; water resistance does not equal slip resistance.
  • Pack a genuinely waterproof alternative for sustained exposure.

How to improve water resistance safely

  1. Identify the upper: smooth full-grain leather, waxed nubuck, plain nubuck or suede.
  2. Remove dry dirt with a soft brush or appropriate cloth.
  3. Let the boot dry fully at room temperature.
  4. Test the treatment on an inconspicuous area because colour can change.
  5. Apply an appropriate spray, wax or cream evenly and follow its instructions.

Blundstone recommends waterproofing approximately every three months depending on use, more often in a wetter climate. Frequency is not a reason to overload leather with product; condition and manufacturer instructions come first.

What to do if Blundstones get soaked

Remove the footbeds, wipe away dirt and let everything dry naturally in a ventilated room. Do not place the boots on a radiator, use a hairdryer or expose them to strong direct heat. Accelerated heat can dry leather, change its structure and make it brittle.

When fully dry, assess the correct conditioner or protective product for the upper. If the sole separates, the leather cracks deeply or water enters through a structural fault, stop relying on treatment and speak to the retailer.

Frequently asked questions

Are Blundstone boots waterproof?

Most leather Blundstones are water-resistant rather than fully waterproof. The manufacturer says leather can repel normal rain but does not recommend prolonged exposure to moisture.

Can I wear Blundstones in the rain?

Yes, water-resistant models can handle ordinary showers and wet pavements when in good condition and correctly maintained. Avoid deep water and prolonged saturation.

How often should I waterproof Blundstones?

Blundstone recommends about every three months depending on use, with more frequent treatment in wetter conditions. Always use a product suitable for the specific leather.

Which Blundstone is best for wet winter weather?

A Thermal model such as the #566 has a more weather-focused specification, including treated leather, coated elastic, sealed seams, Thinsulate insulation and a sheepswool footbed.

Can Blundstones get wet in the rain?

Brief rain is different from soaking. Check the model’s weather specification, let wet leather dry naturally and choose purpose-built waterproof footwear when sustained exposure is expected.
