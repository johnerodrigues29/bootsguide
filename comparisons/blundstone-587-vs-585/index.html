Blundstone 587 vs 585: What's the Difference?

The Blundstone 587 and 585 are effectively the same Classics Chelsea boot in two rustic colours. This comparison shows where the specifications match, how Rustic Black and Rustic Brown change the look, and which one makes more sense for your wardrobe.

By BootsGuide EditorialPublished and fact-checked 11 September 2026
Quick answer

Choose by colour: their published specifications otherwise match

Buy the Blundstone 587 if you want a dark Rustic Black finish that works easily with black denim, grey trousers and understated outfits. Buy the Blundstone 585 if you prefer a warmer Rustic Brown boot with more obvious tonal variation. Blundstone lists the same waxed-nubuck upper type, relaxed Classics fit, lining, XRD® footbed, sole materials and £185 UK price for both models when checked.

What the 587 and 585 have in common

These are not two different performance tiers. Both sit in Blundstone's Classics series and the manufacturer publishes the same core construction for each model. That means neither has a meaningful technical advantage in underfoot cushioning, fit family, outsole design or stated weather resistance.

Each boot is lined with leather and textile and includes a removable contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD® technology. Beneath it, Blundstone specifies a TPU outsole, PU midsole and SPS shock-protection system. The practical implication is simple: if one colour is available at a better retailer price, that saving is not buying a lower specification.

Both also use a pull-on Chelsea design with the familiar front and rear tabs. Neither has laces for extra heel lockdown, a waterproof membrane, winter insulation or the Vibram outsole found on selected All-Terrain models. For everyday city use, travel and firm paths, their strengths and limitations are closely aligned.

The real difference: Rustic Black vs Rustic Brown

Blundstone 587: Rustic Black

The 587 is the darker and quieter option, but “Rustic Black” does not mean a perfectly uniform, polished black. Its waxed nubuck has a soft, slightly mottled surface that can show grey and charcoal variation in changing light. It looks more casual than a smooth black dress boot while pairing easily with black, navy, grey and neutral clothing.

Blundstone 585: Rustic Brown

The 585 has the warmer and more recognisably rustic appearance. Brown, olive and muted tan tones can emerge across the waxed surface, so it works naturally with blue denim, khaki, corduroy and workwear-inspired layers. It makes more of a visual statement than the 587 without becoming a bright brown boot.

Blundstone notes that its leathers are kept close to their natural state, so colour variation between individual pairs is expected. Retailer photography should therefore guide your choice, not promise an exact shade.

Which finish ages better?

Neither finish is inherently more durable because both use the same stated upper material. The visible ageing is what changes. Marks on the 585 tend to join an already varied brown surface, creating an obvious casual patina. The 587 may hide dark dirt more readily, but pale dust and dry-looking areas can stand out against its darker base.

The best-looking option after months of wear is the one whose changing character you already like. Choose the 585 if you actively want tonal variation and a worn-in brown appearance. Choose the 587 if you want the same texture in a darker, more subdued colour. Do not expect either rustic finish to stay perfectly uniform.

Fit and sizing: no reason to choose differently

Blundstone describes both models as true to size with a relaxed fit from the start. The Classics shape gives more room across the top and toe than the firmer Originals, while an extra set of footbeds lets you reduce internal volume when needed.

Choose a full size for length and use a half size when you need more width. Blundstone half sizes increase space over the foot rather than making the boot longer. Aim for roughly 1 cm of room in front of the toes, expect a small amount of heel lift and try the boots with the socks you normally wear.

Leather variation and individual feet can still make two physical pairs feel slightly different. However, there is no published basis for sizing a 587 differently from a 585. If your current 585 fits well, the equivalent 587 size is the logical starting point.

Comfort and walking performance: a tie

Both models have the same leather-and-textile lining, removable cushioned footbed and Classics sole construction. Their underfoot feel should therefore be very similar when size, socks and footbed setup match. The relaxed upper can suit long days of ordinary standing and urban walking, while the XRD® component is positioned to manage impact at the heel.

Colour does not change support. For steep, loose or muddy terrain, neither boot gains the secure lacing, high ankle structure or terrain-specific tread of a technical walking boot. If grip is the priority, compare the Blundstone 2056 with the 585 instead.

Weather resistance and care are the same

The 587 and 585 use water-resistant waxed nubuck, not a fully waterproof membrane. Both are reasonable for everyday UK showers and wet pavements when maintained, but neither should be treated as a substitute for a waterproof boot during prolonged saturation, deep puddles or repeated muddy use.

Blundstone groups the 585 and 587 together in its waxed-nubuck care guidance. Remove dirt with a damp cloth, allow the boots to air dry away from heaters, then use Rustic Renovating Cream and waterproof spray as directed. Test any product on a hidden area first because treatment may alter colour or appearance. Our Blundstone cleaning guide explains the process in more detail.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Blundstone 587 and 585?

The published UK specifications are the same apart from colour: the 587 is Rustic Black and the 585 is Rustic Brown. Both use waxed nubuck, the Classics fit, leather-and-textile lining, removable XRD footbed and the same stated sole construction.

Do Blundstone 587 and 585 fit the same?

Yes. Both belong to the Classics series and receive the same true-to-size, relaxed-fit guidance. Half sizes add width rather than length.

Are the Blundstone 587 and 585 waterproof?

No. Both use water-resistant waxed nubuck and suit ordinary rain when maintained, but neither model is presented as fully waterproof for prolonged saturation or deep water.

Should I buy the Blundstone 587 or 585?

Choose the 587 for a dark, versatile Rustic Black finish or the 585 for a warmer Rustic Brown finish with more visible tonal variation. Fit, comfort and stated performance should not decide between them.
