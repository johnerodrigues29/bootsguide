Blundstone 558 vs 585: Which Classic Should You Buy?

The Blundstone 558 and 585 share the same lined Classics platform, £185 official UK price and relaxed fit. The decision is about leather and appearance: smooth black full-grain leather on the 558 or Rustic Brown waxed nubuck on the 585.

By BootsGuide Editorial25 September 2026
Quick answer

Choose the 558 for a cleaner black look; choose the 585 for rustic character

Buy the Blundstone 558 if you want the more versatile black option, a smoother surface and a boot that can move more easily between casual and smart-casual outfits. Buy the Blundstone 585 if you prefer warm brown tones, visible leather variation and a more relaxed workwear aesthetic.

There is no published fit or comfort advantage between them. Both belong to the Classics series and use the same lining, removable XRD® footbed, TPU outsole, PU midsole and SPS shock-protection system. At the same retailer price, leather preference should decide.

What the 558 and 585 have in common

These models occupy the same position in the Blundstone range. Each is a lined Classic rather than an unlined Original, so both receive the brand’s more relaxed fit guidance across the top and toe area. Both include an extra pair of footbeds to help reduce internal volume when the length is right but the boot feels loose.

Underfoot, their manufacturer specifications are identical: a removable contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD® technology, a PU midsole, a TPU outsole and the SPS shock-protection system. Both are pull-on Chelsea boots with elastic side panels and front and rear pull tabs. Neither provides the precise heel lockdown of laces, the insulation of the Thermal 566 or the Vibram outsole used by the All-Terrain 2056.

That shared construction makes this a more straightforward decision than the 500 vs 585. You are not choosing between two fit families or paying for a different comfort package. You are choosing how the upper looks, ages and should be maintained.

The decisive difference: smooth leather vs waxed nubuck

Blundstone 558: smooth black full-grain leather

The 558 has a uniform black upper with a smoother surface and lower visual contrast. It looks tidier with black denim, grey trousers, dark workwear and simple smart-casual outfits. The colour also makes it easier to wear the same boot across commuting, travel and informal office settings.

Black is not maintenance-free. Dust, dried road salt and pale scuffs can stand out, while heavy polish can change the sheen. The advantage is that compatible black or neutral smooth-leather products can restore a more even appearance when used carefully.

Blundstone 585: Rustic Brown waxed nubuck

The 585 is deliberately less uniform. Its waxed nubuck can show brown, olive and muted tan variation, and Blundstone notes that natural colour differences between pairs are expected. That character suits blue denim, khaki, corduroy and layered casual clothing particularly well.

Marks often blend into the 585’s mottled surface rather than looking like damage, but treatment can darken or even out the leather. Buyers who want every panel to stay one precise shade may find the 558 easier to live with; buyers who enjoy patina are more likely to prefer the 585.

Fit and sizing: expect the same result

Blundstone describes both boots as true to UK size and relaxed from the outset. The Classics last has more room across the instep and toe area than the firmer Originals. For length, aim for roughly 1 cm between the longest toe and the front of the boot. A small amount of heel movement is normal in a pull-on design, but painful instep pressure or toes contacting the front should not be treated as ordinary break-in.

Blundstone half-size rule: a half size adds width and volume, not length. Move up a full size when you need more toe length; try the half size when the length works but the boot feels tight across the foot.

The extra footbeds supplied with Classics models provide another adjustment. Adding the supplementary footbed can reduce volume and heel movement; removing it creates more space. Try both models indoors on carpet with the socks you normally wear. If one physical pair feels different, leather variation or manufacturing tolerance is more likely than a model-specific sizing rule.

Comfort and walking performance: effectively a tie

Fit has a greater effect on comfort than the colour or surface treatment of the upper. Both models use the same relaxed shape, leather-and-textile lining, removable EVA footbed with XRD® at the heel and Classics sole system. When size, socks and footbed setup match, their underfoot feel should be very similar.

The 558’s smooth full-grain leather and the 585’s waxed nubuck may soften and crease differently, but neither material creates a published cushioning advantage. Both are most convincing for commuting, travel, standing and everyday urban walking. They are less suitable for steep, loose or muddy terrain because a low pull-on boot cannot match the ankle control and adjustable fit of a technical laced walking boot.

If your shortlist is based on walking performance alone, buy whichever upper fits your wardrobe or is available at the better price. If outsole grip on mixed surfaces is the priority, read our 2056 vs 585 comparison instead.

Weather resistance is similar, but care is not identical

Both uppers are described as water-resistant rather than waterproof. Maintained leather should cope with ordinary UK showers, damp pavements and splashes, but water can eventually enter around the elastic panels or the top opening. Neither is intended for prolonged saturation, standing water or repeated deep mud.

The care routine must match the leather. For the 558, remove dirt gently, air-dry away from direct heat and use a compatible product for smooth full-grain leather. For the 585, use products intended for waxed nubuck, such as Blundstone’s Rustic Renovating Cream, only after checking the instructions. Always test treatments on a discreet area because they can change colour and finish.

Do not use the same product on both boots merely because the construction and price match. A polish designed to create shine on smooth black leather can flatten the 585’s rustic texture; a rustic-leather conditioner may not produce the clean finish expected on the 558. Our Blundstone cleaning guide separates the routines by material.

Which model will age better?

Durability cannot be ranked from colour alone. Both use premium leather uppers on the same sole construction, and service life depends more on fit, use, drying, cleaning and outsole wear. What changes is the visibility of ageing.

The 558 tends to show contrast: light scratches, dust and salt are noticeable against black, but careful smooth-leather treatment can return the upper to a more uniform appearance. The 585 accepts visual variation from the beginning, so scuffs and tonal changes become part of its casual patina. It may look “better worn” to someone who likes rustic leather, while appearing inconsistent to someone who prefers a neat finish.

Choose according to the ageing you want, not the hope that one colour is inherently tougher. Regularly soaked leather, forced drying and poor fit can shorten the useful life of either model.

Price and value: should one cost more?

Blundstone listed both the 558 and 585 at £185 in the UK when checked on 25 September 2026. At equal prices, neither has a stronger specification-based value claim: the fit family, lining, footbed and sole package match. The better value is simply the finish you will wear more often.

Retailer prices, sizes and sellers can differ. A modest discount can reasonably decide the purchase if you like both colours equally, because choosing the cheaper listing does not mean accepting an inferior comfort system. Confirm the exact model number, colour and seller before ordering, especially when marketplace listings group several variants on one page.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between Blundstone 558 and 585?

The 558 uses smooth black full-grain leather, while the 585 uses Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. Their published Classics fit, lining, footbed and sole specifications otherwise match.

Do the Blundstone 558 and 585 fit the same?

Yes. Both are Classics models with the same relaxed, true-to-size guidance. A half size adds width and volume rather than extra length.

Is the Blundstone 558 smarter than the 585?

The smooth black 558 usually looks cleaner and smarter, while the mottled Rustic Brown 585 has a more casual, outdoors-oriented appearance. Both remain everyday Chelsea boots rather than formal footwear.

Are the Blundstone 558 and 585 waterproof?

No. Both have water-resistant leather uppers but no fully waterproof membrane. They suit routine showers and wet pavements when maintained, not prolonged saturation or deep water.

Should I buy the Blundstone 558 or 585?

Choose the 558 for smooth black leather, a cleaner appearance and simple dark-colour matching. Choose the 585 for a warmer Rustic Brown finish, visible tonal variation and a casual patina. Fit and comfort should be effectively equal when the size is the same.
