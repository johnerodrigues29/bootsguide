Blundstone 1609 vs 550: Which Brown Classic Should You Choose?

Two brown Classics, a very similar specification and one main decision: which finish would you choose to wear? Here is where the 1609 and 550 differ, and where paying more brings no demonstrated advantage.

By BootsGuide EditorialPrepared and source-checked 14 October 2026
Quick answer

Choose the finish first; use the price to break a tie

Our starting recommendation is the 550 if you like both brown finishes equally. Choose the 1609 when Antique Brown is the one you actually want to wear. Paying more for a colour you prefer can make sense; paying more because you assume the higher model number means better walking performance does not.

Choose the 1609

You prefer the Antique Brown look and can justify any difference in the price of your size.

Choose the 550

You want Walnut Brown, or it delivers the better offer without giving up anything important to your use.

Antique Brown or Walnut Brown?

This is a closer decision than 1609 vs 585, where a rustic nubuck finish enters the picture. Here, shortlist the colour that works with the trousers and outerwear you already own. Our visual preference is to use the 1609 when we want more colour character, and the 550 when we want a quieter brown foundation.

Those are styling judgements, not laboratory findings about leather quality. Product photos, screen settings, lighting and care all affect how brown looks. Compare more than one photograph, and assess the delivered pair in daylight before wearing it outside.

Do not let “brown Blundstone” stand in for an exact model. If you chose Antique Brown after looking at the 1609, confirm #1609 at checkout. A Walnut Brown 550 is not a substitute simply because both are Classics.

Fit and comfort: start with your Classics size

Both official product pages recommend the usual size, with a half size for extra width. The general Blundstone size guide is the useful next step if you have never tried the brand.

For an owner happy in a 550, we would begin a 1609 fitting with that size. For a new buyer, compare length, instep pressure and heel security in the actual pair instead of using a familiar trainer size as a guarantee.

A little heel movement can be normal in a Chelsea. The distinction is whether it feels controlled during walking or whether your foot slides around. Do not choose an excessively loose pair simply to avoid the sensation of new leather, and do not accept toe contact in the hope that wear will add length.

We have no side-by-side wear-test data showing one model reduces fatigue more than the other. The shared specifications give us no reason to promise a comfort advantage for the dearer pair.

Everyday use, rain and terrain

The water-resistant uppers make these everyday candidates rather than a basis for a waterproof claim. If your shopping brief is a wet commute with occasional showers, assess fit and care needs alongside the finish. If it is sustained rain, saturated grass or deep puddles, change the shortlist to boots with explicit waterproof specifications.

Neither purchase should be justified by an assumed upgrade in grip over the other. For mixed outdoor surfaces, our 2056 All-Terrain review explains a different outsole option. A demanding hike may call for lacing and a purpose-built hiking design instead.

Care: preserve the finish you chose

Blundstone’s full-grain care guidance starts with gentle dirt removal, air drying away from heat and testing any cream or polish on a discreet area. Do that with either pair before applying a new product across the visible upper.

Our advice is to avoid treating colour change as a surprise after the purchase: if the appeal of the 1609 is its finish, a heavily pigmented polish may move it away from the look you wanted. Follow the care-product instructions and use the appropriate material guidance, rather than copying a routine for suede or plain nubuck.

For a fuller routine, see how to clean Blundstone boots. We cannot predict either pair’s lifespan from its model number, and regular care is not a guarantee of a particular number of years.

Is the 1609 worth the extra £10?

At the checked UK list prices, the gap is £10. In our view that is a reasonable styling premium if Antique Brown is your clear preference. If you feel indifferent when looking at both, keep the money and choose the 550, provided the fit is right.

Retailer prices can reverse the decision. Compare the same size, the exact model, delivery costs and return terms before choosing. We have intentionally avoided a live Amazon price table because an offer on one size does not establish the price of another.

Already own a brown Classic that fits well? We would not suggest buying the other solely for a functional upgrade. Add a second pair only if you want the different look or have a practical reason for rotating footwear.

Frequently asked questions

Is the 1609 more comfortable than the 550?

There is no clear comfort upgrade in the official specifications compared here. Fit in the actual pair matters more than the higher model number.

Is Antique Brown the same colour as Walnut Brown?

No. They are separate finishes. Use the model code as well as the colour name when checking a retailer listing.

Which is better value?

At the official UK prices checked on 14 October 2026, the 550 is £185 and the 1609 £195. Our default value pick is the 550 if you like both equally; compare the final retailer price in your correct size.

Can I move from a 550 to a 1609 in the same size?

Your existing Classics size is a sensible starting point, but try the actual pair indoors. Do not change size only because the colour or model number changes.

Which should I choose for heavy rain?

Neither is our first choice when waterproof protection is essential. Both are everyday Classics with water-resistant leather; that is not a waterproof guarantee.
