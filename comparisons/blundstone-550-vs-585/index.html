Blundstone 550 vs 585: Which Should You Buy?

The useful choice comes down to the stated leather, lining, colour and fit details that matter to your own intended use—not a generic winner.

Quick answer

Choose by the detail that affects your use.

Use the 550 route if its current materials and appearance better fit your preferences. Use the 585 route if the Classic-series brown leather specification and everyday context align more closely with what you need. Verify current retailer specifications before buying.

What the comparison should focus on

Leather, colour and style

Leather finish and colour affect both the look of a boot and how it may fit into an existing wardrobe. Use current images and specifications rather than relying on names alone, as retailer listings may show variations.

Comfort and fit

Neither model name can answer a fit question. Check the toe shape, instep room, heel hold and sock thickness you expect to use. A returns policy is valuable when fit is uncertain.

Weather suitability

For either boot, distinguish between a boot that suits a damp everyday commute and footwear intended for sustained wet terrain. Care, leather treatment and actual exposure materially change the answer.

Which route may suit you?

Choose the 550 if…

  • The current 550 materials and finish better match your preferences.
  • You have checked the retailer’s fit and lining information.
  • Its appearance works better for your expected use.

Choose the 585 if…

  • You prefer the current 585 brown leather specification.
  • You want the Classic-series context for casual, everyday use.
  • You have confirmed sizing and returns information.
