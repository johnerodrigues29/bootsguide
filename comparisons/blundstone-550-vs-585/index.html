Blundstone 550 vs 585: Which Should You Buy?

These two Classics share most of their construction. The practical choice comes down to upper material, colour, care and the finish you want to live with.

Quick answer

The material is the decision

Choose the 550 if you prefer a smoother Walnut Brown full-grain leather that can look more polished. Choose the 585 if you prefer the textured, naturally varied appearance of Rustic Brown waxed nubuck.

Fit, lining, footbed and sole specifications are otherwise closely matched, so neither model is a universal upgrade over the other.

Leather & colour

The 550 uses Walnut Brown full-grain leather. Its smoother face is the stronger fit for someone who wants a cleaner, more polished-looking boot. The 585 uses Rustic Brown waxed nubuck, which has a softer-looking texture and more obvious natural colour variation.

Neither finish is inherently better. The 550’s appeal is visual consistency and a smarter lean; the 585’s appeal is a finish that looks relaxed from the start and can wear marks as character.

Comfort & fit

The published comfort components are effectively the same: both sit in the Classics series, use leather-and-textile lining and include a removable contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD technology. Both official model pages describe the fit as true to size and suggest moving up half a size for additional width.

Remember that Blundstone half sizing affects width and instep volume rather than length. The shared specification makes foot shape and correct sizing more important than model number for comfort.

Lining & outsole

Both models use the same published lining combination and Classic sole unit, with a TPU outsole, PU midsole and the brand’s shock-protection system. On those criteria, there is no meaningful paper advantage to either model.

Weather suitability

Both uppers are described as water-resistant. Neither standard model should be treated as fully waterproof, and the brand advises against prolonged moisture exposure. The more important difference is care: products suitable for smooth full-grain leather may not be right for waxed nubuck, and any treatment can affect appearance.

Style

The 550 can move a little more easily towards smart-casual outfits because of the smoother Walnut Brown upper. The 585 leans more relaxed and outdoorsy through its mottled Rustic Brown finish. Shape and sole are closely related, so material does most of the visual work.

Price & value

Retail prices change and are therefore not hard-coded here. When the price is similar, choose by finish and care preference. If one model is discounted, decide whether the saving is worth accepting a material you did not originally want; a bargain is poor value if the finish never feels right.

Final verdict

The 550 and 585 are siblings, not steps on a ladder. The 550’s smooth full-grain leather is the more polished option; the 585’s waxed nubuck is the more visibly rustic one. With the major comfort and sole specifications aligned, choosing the finish you actually prefer is the rational answer.
