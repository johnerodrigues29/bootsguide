Blundstone 550 vs 585: Every Difference Explained

The #550 and #585 are closer than most comparisons suggest. They share the same Classics comfort platform; the real choice is smooth full-grain leather versus rustic waxed nubuck.

Quick answer

Choose the leather, not a supposed performance winner

Buy the Blundstone 550 if you want smoother Walnut Brown full-grain leather with a cleaner smart-casual appearance. Buy the Blundstone 585 if you prefer mottled Rustic Brown waxed nubuck that develops visible character. Both use the relaxed Classics fit, leather-and-textile lining, removable XRD® footbed and the same stated sole construction.

The deciding factor: leather and colour

Blundstone 550: smooth Walnut Brown

The 550 uses full-grain leather with a smoother surface. It is easier to dress up with chinos, knitwear or a relaxed office wardrobe, and suitable leather cream can restore a more polished appearance. Scratches may look sharper initially than on mottled nubuck.

Blundstone 585: Rustic Brown waxed nubuck

The 585 uses waxed nubuck, which gives the boot a varied, worn-in-looking surface. Natural colour differences are expected, and the pair develops character rather than maintaining a uniform polished finish. That makes it a natural match for denim and casual workwear.

If you are viewing both through a small retailer thumbnail, the differences can look subtle. In person, surface texture and the way each leather reflects light are much more noticeable.

Fit, comfort and sizing: effectively a tie

Both models belong to the Classics series and receive the same official fit guidance: true to size, relaxed over the top and toe, with a worn-in feel from the first wear. Both include extra footbeds to personalise volume.

Use full sizes for length and half sizes for extra width. A UK 8.5 is not longer than an 8 in the way many shoppers expect. Allow about 1 cm in front of the toes, tolerate only slight heel lift and try the boots with your normal socks.

Because leather is natural and feet are not identical, “same last” does not guarantee two individual pairs will feel indistinguishable. Fit both if you are genuinely between them.

Care and wet-weather use

Both uppers are water-resistant rather than fully waterproof. They are reasonable choices for ordinary UK showers and wet pavements when maintained, but neither is intended for prolonged saturation.

Care for the 550

Brush or wipe away dirt, air dry naturally and use a suitable smooth-leather cream or polish after testing a hidden area.

Care for the 585

Use products intended for waxed nubuck. Blundstone specifically points owners towards non-pigmented Rustic Renovating Cream and waterproof spray.

Do not dry either model on a radiator. Concentrated heat can make leather dry and brittle.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Blundstone 550 or 585?

Neither is universally better. Choose the 550 for smooth Walnut Brown full-grain leather and the 585 for Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. Their core Classics fit, lining, footbed and sole specifications are otherwise very similar.

Do the Blundstone 550 and 585 fit the same?

Both are in the Classics series and Blundstone gives them the same relaxed-fit guidance. Natural leather variation and individual feet can still make two pairs feel slightly different.

Which is easier to care for?

Both need regular care, but the products differ. Smooth full-grain leather on the 550 accepts suitable leather cream; the 585 needs products intended for waxed nubuck, such as non-pigmented Rustic Renovating Cream.

Which looks smarter?

The 550’s smoother Walnut Brown surface generally reads as smarter, while the 585’s mottled Rustic Brown finish is more casual and outdoors-influenced.
