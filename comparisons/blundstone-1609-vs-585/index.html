Blundstone 1609 vs 585: Antique Brown or Rustic Brown?

Two brown Classics, two different leather finishes. Here is how to choose between the 1609 and 585 without paying for a comfort upgrade their specifications do not establish.

By BootsGuide EditorialPrepared and fact-checked 9 October 2026
Quick answer

1609 for Antique Brown; 585 for the rustic finish

Choose the 1609 if its waxed full-grain appearance suits your wardrobe. Choose the 585 if you prefer mottled Rustic Brown waxed nubuck. Both are Classics: this is primarily a leather and styling decision, rather than a clear upgrade in underfoot construction.

Antique Brown or Rustic Brown?

1609: our pick for a less rustic wardrobe

Our styling preference is the 1609 for outfits built around chinos, an overshirt or understated knitwear. Think about whether you want the boots to blend into the outfit or provide a visibly weathered detail. This is an editorial judgement about appearance, not a claim of more formal construction.

585: our pick when texture is the attraction

The 585 has the more visibly varied finish in the product photographs. We would shortlist it for denim and relaxed weekend clothing. Blundstone explicitly notes natural colour variation, so do not expect every pair to match a retailer thumbnail exactly.

If you already own one model, ask what the other would add to your wardrobe. A second shade may be worthwhile, but buying both is difficult to justify purely as a performance upgrade. For individual detail, see the 1609 review and 585 review.

Sizing and comfort: start with the same fit checks

Blundstone describes the Classics as relaxed across the instep and toes. Its half sizes add width and volume, not length: moving from a UK 8 to 8.5 is therefore not the solution if your toes need more length.

Try each pair standing and walking indoors with the socks you expect to wear. A little heel movement can be normal; painful pressure or toes hitting the front should not be dismissed as break-in. The leather may give over the instep, but it will not stretch lengthwise.

Our buying advice: if you know your size in one, use it as a starting point for the other, then assess the actual pair. Read the UK size guide before choosing a different size just because a seller has it on offer.

Different care products; the same caution in rain

For the 1609, follow full-grain leather care: remove dirt gently, air dry and use a suitable leather-care product after a small hidden-area test. For the 585, follow the waxed-nubuck instructions; Blundstone recommends its non-pigmented Rustic Renovating Cream and waterproof spray for this finish. Do not confuse coated waxed nubuck with plain nubuck.

Either finish can change after treatment, so test before covering the whole boot. Let wet boots dry naturally away from radiators and direct heat. Our cleaning guide explains the material-specific routines.

Neither product page makes these models a fully waterproof boot. If extended wet-weather use is your main requirement, use our waterproofing guide to reassess the shortlist rather than choosing between these two colours.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Blundstone 1609 an upgrade over the 585?

There is no clear upgrade in the listed comfort platform. Choose according to the finish you prefer and the offer in your correct size.

Should I change size between the 1609 and 585?

Do not change size purely because the model number is different. Start with your usual Classics fit and assess the actual pair with your normal socks.

Can I use the same cream on both?

Check compatibility with each material. Follow full-grain guidance for the 1609 and waxed-nubuck guidance for the 585; test any product on a hidden area first.

Which would BootsGuide choose?

For a less rustic outfit we would shortlist the 1609; for a textured casual look, the 585. These are styling preferences, not results from a hands-on comparison.
