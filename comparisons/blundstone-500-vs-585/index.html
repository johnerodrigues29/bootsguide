Blundstone 500 vs 585: Original or Classic?

The #500 and #585 may share the same broad silhouette, but they are meaningfully different underfoot and over the instep. The choice is firm, unlined simplicity versus a roomier, lined comfort package.

By BootsGuide EditorialFact-checked and updated 21 August 2026
Quick answer

The 585 is easier from day one; the 500 is simpler

Choose the 500 if you want the core unlined Original, like a firmer new-boot fit and prefer smooth Stout Brown leather. Choose the 585 if you want more room over the foot, a lining, a softer rustic upper and the upgraded XRD® comfort footbed.

Fit and break-in are the biggest differences

The 500 starts firm across the instep and toe. Its thicker unlined leather is expected to soften and mould with use. The 585 belongs to the Classics series, which Blundstone describes as more relaxed and lined for comfort from the outset.

Both generally run true to UK size according to the manufacturer. In both, a half size changes width rather than length. Buyers with a high instep or wider forefoot are more likely to find the 585 accommodating, although no model-level advice replaces trying the boot.

Do not size up to manufacture comfort. First establish length, then compare the corresponding half size for width or use the extra Classics footbed to tune volume.

Leather, lining and underfoot feel

Blundstone 500

Smooth Stout Brown full-grain leather, no lining and a removable contoured EVA footbed. The feel is simpler and firmer.

Blundstone 585

Rustic Brown waxed nubuck, leather-and-textile lining and a removable contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD® technology.

The 500’s smooth leather suits buyers who prefer a traditional, cleaner surface. The 585’s nubuck is deliberately irregular and more casual. Care products are not interchangeable: use full-grain leather care for the 500 and waxed-nubuck-compatible care for the 585.

Rain, winter and walking

Both can handle normal light rain when cared for, but neither is a fully waterproof boot for prolonged exposure. Their pull-on construction also limits ankle lockdown for hiking. For everyday urban walking, either can work; the 585’s softer fit is likely to be easier immediately, while a correctly fitted 500 may become very comfortable after softening.

For regular muddy paths, compare the Vibram-equipped All-Terrain #2056. For cold and wetter winter use, the #566 Thermal adds coated elastic, sealed seams, Thinsulate® and a sheepswool footbed.

Value and long-term choice

The 500 is the more economical path into the core design. The 585 justifies its premium through the lining, relaxed fit and more advanced footbed rather than a dramatic change in waterproofing or outdoor capability. Pay for the comfort features only if they match your feet and use.

Which one should you buy?

Buy the 500 if…

  • You prefer a firmer, unlined boot.
  • You want smooth Stout Brown leather.
  • You are comfortable with gradual softening.
  • You value the simpler, lower-priced Original.

Frequently asked questions

Which is more comfortable, Blundstone 500 or 585?

The 585 has the more comfort-focused specification: relaxed Classics fit, leather-and-textile lining and an XRD-equipped footbed. Some buyers still prefer the simpler unlined feel of the 500 once it has softened.

Do the Blundstone 500 and 585 fit the same?

No. The 500 Original is firmer over the top and toe when new. The 585 Classic is more relaxed and comes with extra footbeds for volume adjustment.

Which needs less breaking in?

The 585 is described as having a worn-in feel from the first wear. The 500’s thicker, unlined full-grain leather generally needs more time to soften.

Which is better in rain?

Both have water-resistant leather and neither should be treated as fully waterproof. Care and condition matter more than choosing one of these two for prolonged wet use.
