Blundstone 1306 vs 585: Which Should You Choose?

The Blundstone 1306 and 585 share Rustic Brown waxed nubuck and the same £185 official UK price, but they are built for different priorities. The 1306 is the Dress Series chisel-toe boot; the 585 is the rounder, more casual Classics option.

By BootsGuide EditorialPublished and fact-checked 2 October 2026
Quick answer

Choose by shape first: chisel-toe 1306 or rounder 585

Choose the Blundstone 1306 if you want a leaner, smarter-looking Chelsea boot with a chisel toe and Dress Series sole. Choose the Blundstone 585 if you prefer the familiar rounded Blundstone silhouette, a relaxed Classics fit and a more casual everyday appearance.

Both are Rustic Brown waxed nubuck, pull-on Chelsea boots and both were listed at £185 on the official UK store when checked for this comparison. The biggest practical difference is fit: Blundstone says the 1306 runs longer and wider, while the 585 is true to size and relaxed.

What the 1306 and 585 have in common

These boots share more than their Rustic Brown colour. Both are unisex pull-on Chelsea boots with elastic side panels, pull tabs, water-resistant leather uppers and removable footbeds. Both use Blundstone’s SPS shock-protection system and durable TPU-based outsole construction.

Neither is a waterproof hiking boot. Both make more sense for commuting, travel, everyday errands, casual offices and general urban walking than for deep mud, technical trails or situations where a lace-adjustable ankle fit is important.

The shared leather also means natural variation is part of the buying experience. Blundstone notes that its leathers are kept close to their natural state, so colour and appearance can vary between pairs and change with wear.

1306 vs 585: the shape is the biggest difference

Blundstone 1306: chisel toe and Dress profile

The 1306 has a more elongated, angular front. Blundstone describes the chisel toe as a refined profile designed for day-to-night versatility. The result is visually closer to a smart Chelsea boot than the familiar rounded Blundstone shape.

Blundstone 585: the classic rounded silhouette

The 585 is immediately recognisable as a Classics model. Its rounded toe, pull-on proportions and Rustic Brown finish give it the more traditional Blundstone appearance. It works naturally with jeans, chinos, workwear and other casual combinations.

Fit and sizing: this is where buyers should pay attention

The 1306 and 585 should not be treated as identical fits simply because they share the same colour and brand. Blundstone UK says the 1306 has a longer and wider fit and suggests considering a half or even full size down, particularly for narrow feet. The 585 is described as true to size with a relaxed fit.

Important: the 1306’s extra front space comes partly from its chisel-toe construction. Do not size down automatically without checking heel security, instep pressure and actual toe position. Try the boot indoors with your normal socks and use the retailer’s return policy as part of the sizing decision.

For the 585, Blundstone says a half size is used for a wider fit rather than additional length. That makes the 585 easier to interpret if the standard size gives the correct toe length but feels snug across the foot. Our Blundstone size guide explains the general sizing system in more detail.

Comfort and walking: different fit, similar everyday role

Both boots are designed for everyday comfort, but the comfort package is not identical. The 585 has a contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD® technology and is positioned in Blundstone’s Classics range. The 1306 uses a contoured EVA footbed and Dress Series construction with SPS shock protection.

For ordinary pavement walking, commuting and travel, either can work well when correctly fitted. The decision becomes more personal around foot shape. A wearer who likes a rounder, more relaxed boot may prefer the 585, while someone who finds the elongated chisel shape comfortable and wants a sharper silhouette may prefer the 1306.

Neither should be confused with the All-Terrain 2056, which uses a Vibram outsole for a different traction brief. If mixed-surface grip is the deciding factor, that model deserves consideration instead.

Which is better for smart-casual outfits?

The 1306 has the clearer advantage if your wardrobe includes smarter trousers, tailored denim, knitwear, overshirts or casual office clothing. Its chisel toe and slimmer visual line make the boot look less rugged than the standard Classics shape.

The 585 is more naturally casual. Its rounded toe and Rustic Brown character pair easily with denim, chinos, flannel, corduroy and workwear. It can still look polished, but its strength is versatility within casual dressing rather than trying to imitate a formal Chelsea boot.

Neither is a formal dress shoe. The 1306 is simply the more refined of the two shapes.

Rain, care and weather resistance

Both models use water-resistant Rustic Brown waxed nubuck rather than a fully waterproof construction. They are appropriate for ordinary British rain and wet pavements when cared for correctly, but prolonged soaking, deep puddles and standing water are outside that claim.

Because the uppers are the same material family, care should be broadly similar. Avoid aggressive cleaning that strips the waxed finish, allow wet boots to dry naturally away from direct heat, and use a compatible leather treatment sparingly. Our Blundstone cleaning guide covers material-specific care.

How the two Rustic Brown boots age

Neither model should be expected to stay visually identical to its product photography. Blundstone explicitly notes natural colour variation, and waxed nubuck develops character with wear.

The difference is more about silhouette than leather ageing. The 1306’s angular toe continues to make the boot look relatively smart as the leather develops marks and variation. The 585’s rounded profile leans into a more relaxed, lived-in appearance. If you want one pair that can move between casual and smarter settings, that distinction may matter more than the leather itself.

Price and value: both were £185 in the UK

Blundstone’s UK store listed both the 1306 and 585 at £185 when checked on 2 October 2026. At the same official price, the purchase decision is less about getting “more boot” and more about choosing the shape and fit family that suits you.

Retail prices, stock and sizes can change. If one model is discounted materially, that can reasonably affect the decision—provided the exact model, colour and size are confirmed before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

What is the main difference between the Blundstone 1306 and 585?

The 1306 is a Dress Series boot with a longer, wider chisel-toe profile and Dress sole. The 585 is a Classics boot with a rounder profile, relaxed fit and Classic sole. Both use Rustic Brown waxed nubuck and were listed at £185 in the UK when checked.

Do the Blundstone 1306 and 585 fit the same?

No. Blundstone gives the 1306 longer and wider fit guidance and suggests considering a half or full size down, especially for narrow feet. The 585 is described as true to size and relaxed, with a half size used for more width.

Which is more comfortable, the Blundstone 1306 or 585?

Both have SPS shock protection and removable footbeds, but their fit and underfoot packages differ. The 585 uses a contoured EVA footbed with Poron XRD technology, while the 1306 uses a contoured EVA footbed and Dress sole. Comfort depends strongly on which shape fits your foot.

Are the Blundstone 1306 and 585 waterproof?

No. Blundstone describes both as water-resistant rather than fully waterproof. They suit ordinary wet pavements and light rain when maintained, but not prolonged saturation or deep water.

Which should I choose, the Blundstone 1306 or 585?

Choose the 1306 if you want the sharper chisel-toe silhouette and dressier profile. Choose the 585 if you prefer the classic rounded Blundstone shape, relaxed fit and more casual character. Check sizing carefully because the 1306 has different fit guidance.
