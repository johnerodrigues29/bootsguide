Your privacy

Privacy policy

This policy describes the data practices of the static BootsGuide website.

Last updated: 15 August 2026

The current site does not include forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers. This policy will be updated before any such service is enabled.

Information collected automatically

When you visit a website, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, request time, requested page, browser details and security signals in server logs. BootsGuide is intended to be hosted on Cloudflare Pages, so Cloudflare may process limited technical data to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies and local storage

BootsGuide does not currently set first-party cookies or use browser local storage. If analytics, advertising or other third-party features are added later, this policy and any consent mechanism should be updated before those tools are enabled.

Affiliate and external links

Links to retailers or other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices. An affiliate link may include a tracking parameter so a retailer can attribute a qualifying purchase. Review the destination site’s privacy policy before providing information.

Information you provide

BootsGuide currently has no contact, account or newsletter form. If a contact channel is added, the site should explain what information is requested, why it is needed, how long it is retained and who receives it.

Data rights

Applicable privacy rights depend on the site operator, the visitor’s location and the processing performed. When a privacy contact method is added, it will be published here together with the process used to answer requests.

Changes to this policy

This policy should be updated when the website’s data practices change. The “last updated” date should show the most recent material revision.
