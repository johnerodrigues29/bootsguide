Privacy policy

How information is handled on BootsGuide.

This policy describes the current static website and the limited technical data that may be processed when you visit or follow an external link.

ScopeDataCookiesExternal linksRetentionChoicesChanges

Scope

BootsGuide is an informational website. The current site package does not include user accounts, checkout, comments, a newsletter form or a contact form.

Technical data

The hosting and security providers may process routine request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, referring page, time and security signals in order to deliver and protect the website. Their handling is governed by their own policies and the site owner’s configuration.

Cookies and analytics

The current package does not contain a custom analytics or advertising script and does not intentionally set a first-party tracking cookie. Affiliate retailers and external websites may set their own cookies after you follow a link. If analytics or advertising tools are added, this policy and any required consent mechanism should be updated before activation.

External and affiliate links

Links may lead to manufacturers, retailers or affiliate partners. Once you leave BootsGuide, the destination website controls its own data collection, cookies, purchases and account activity. Review that website’s privacy information before providing personal data.

Retention and security

BootsGuide does not maintain a reader database in this site package. Hosting logs, if retained, follow the provider and account configuration. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can block or delete browser cookies, avoid external links and use privacy controls supplied by the destination service. Statutory privacy rights depend on your location and the controller responsible for a particular dataset.

Policy updates

This policy should be reviewed whenever the site adds analytics, advertising, forms, email tools or another data-processing feature. Last updated 20 August 2026.
