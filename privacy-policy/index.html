Privacy policy

How information is handled on BootsGuide.

This policy explains the limited technical and analytics data that may be processed when you visit BootsGuide or follow an external link.

ScopeDataCookiesExternal linksRetentionChoicesChanges

Scope

BootsGuide is an informational website. The current site package does not include user accounts, checkout, comments, a newsletter form or a contact form.

Technical data

The hosting and security providers may process routine request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, referring page, time and security signals in order to deliver and protect the website. Their handling is governed by their own policies and the site owner’s configuration.

When enabled as described below, Google Analytics may process information about the pages you view, referral source, approximate location, device and browser, and interactions used to produce aggregated usage reports.

Cookies and analytics

On individual review and comparison articles displayed at mobile widths (up to 767 pixels), BootsGuide requests Google Analytics approximately three seconds after navigation without requiring prior acceptance, unless you have already rejected analytics. Visitors who leave before it loads may not be measured. Google Analytics may then use analytics cookies. On desktop, hub pages, guides and other pages, Analytics is requested only after acceptance. No acceptance preference is saved merely because the timer has elapsed.

Your cookie preference is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site can remember it. This preference is used for the consent control, not for advertising. Advertising storage and personalisation remain disabled in the site’s Google tag configuration.

External and affiliate links

Links may lead to manufacturers, retailers or affiliate partners. Once you leave BootsGuide, the destination website controls its own data collection, cookies, purchases and account activity. Review that website’s privacy information before providing personal data.

Retention and security

BootsGuide does not maintain a separate reader account database in this site package. Hosting logs and Google Analytics data, if retained, follow the relevant provider and account configuration. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can disable analytics through “Cookie settings” in the footer and select “Reject”, including on mobile articles where the panel does not appear automatically. A saved rejection prevents automatic loading on subsequent pages and disables further Google Analytics measurement on the current page. Previously transmitted data is not withdrawn by this action. To change your selection later, use “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can also block or delete browser cookies, clear local storage, avoid external links and use privacy controls supplied by the destination service. Statutory privacy rights depend on your location and the controller responsible for a particular dataset.

Policy updates

This policy should be reviewed whenever the site changes analytics, advertising, forms, email tools or another data-processing feature. Last updated 10 October 2026.
